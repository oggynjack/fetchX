Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

fetchX is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • URLs you submit: Processed in real-time to fetch video information. Not stored permanently.
  • Downloaded files: Temporarily stored on our server for download, then automatically deleted.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics to improve the service.
  • Download history: Kept only in your own browser (localStorage) so you can re-download recent files. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or included in analytics. Clear it at any time with the Clear button.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to:

  • Process your video download requests
  • Improve service performance and reliability
  • Detect and prevent abuse

3. Data Storage & Security

All downloaded files are automatically deleted from our servers within 1 hour of download. We do not maintain any database of user activity or downloaded content.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: For DDoS protection and security verification
  • yt-dlp: Open-source tool for fetching video information
  • jsDelivr (CDN): Serves the QR-code library, loaded only when you open a QR code. Download links are encoded in your browser, so the URL is never sent to the CDN.

5. Cookies

fetchX uses only essential cookies required for the service to function. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Your download history is stored in browser localStorage (see section 1), not in a cookie, and you can erase it at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect (this policy)
  • Request deletion of any stored data
  • Use the service anonymously

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us through our official channels.
